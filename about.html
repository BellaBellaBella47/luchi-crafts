Hello

Just here to enjoy the making.

Luchi Crafts is my personal knitting portfolio. I wanted somewhere calm and simple to collect photographs of my makes and remember what I have learned along the way.

This isn't a shop and it isn't a business. It is simply a space for my hobby — a visual diary that can grow as I knit more.

A photograph and a few personal details can be added here later if I decide I would like to share more about myself.
